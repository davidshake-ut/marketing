Option 2 · Performance upgrade

Wi-Fi 7 by Ruckus and RG Nets Recommended upgrade path

Muze opens in 2027 and competes into the 2040s. Wi-Fi 7 more than doubles Wi-Fi 6 throughput, adds a clean third band at 6 GHz, and brings wider channels with far less congestion, which matters most in dense AP placements like this one.

  • More than 2x the speed with tri-band radios and multi-gig switching, so the wired side never caps the wireless side.
  • More channels, less congestion. The 6 GHz band is reserved for new devices only. No legacy laptops. No interference from 2018.
  • rXg elevates the resident experience. Carrier-class onboarding portal, per-device personal networks, self-service upgrades, speed tiers the owner can monetize, and PMS integration for automatic move-in and move-out.
  • Wi-Fi 8 ready. We will evaluate Wi-Fi 8 hardware when it arrives later in 2026. Either way, this cabling plant and this gateway carry it without rework.

Faster and more robust, from day one

This option starts with a faster, more robust network from day one, with additional coverage in every 3BR and townhome unit already included.

Option 3 · The full fiber build

XGS-PON: fiber to every home Faster, future-proof, and all managed by Cambium's One Network

Ten gigabits, symmetric, to every unit over a single strand of glass. The telecom closets go passive: no switches, no UPS fleet, nothing to age out. And in every media panel, a 2.5G PoE switch delivers a wired port at the living room TV, powers the ceiling AP, and leaves spare ports for whatever comes next.

  • Higher speed than any copper option. 10G symmetric per unit, with a premium-tier lever built into the splitter design.
  • Future-proofed in glass. The next speed upgrade is a transceiver swap at the headend, not a re-cable of 400 homes.
  • Less to power, less to fail, less to refresh. Passive closets eliminate 18 UPS units and the year-eight switch refresh entirely.
  • Less expensive than the Wi-Fi 7 copper build, with the wired TV port included in every single unit.
